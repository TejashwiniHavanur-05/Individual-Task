## 📘 Task 1: Comparison of Different Forms of Intelligence

### Overview
This project provides a detailed comparison of major intelligence theories and their 
evolution from traditional IQ models to modern multidimensional approaches.

### Theories Covered
- General Intelligence (g-Factor Theory) – Charles Spearman
- Multiple Intelligences Theory – Howard Gardner
- Triarchic Theory – Robert Sternberg
- Emotional Intelligence – Daniel Goleman
- Contribution of Alan Turing to Artificial Intelligence

### Key Concepts
- General Intelligence (g and s factors)
- Types of Multiple Intelligences
- Analytical, Creative, and Practical Intelligence
- Emotional Intelligence in leadership and relationships
- Turing Machine (1936)
- Turing Test (1950)
- Foundations of Artificial Intelligence

### Learning Outcome
Understanding how intelligence evolved from a single measurable ability (IQ) to a multidimensional 
concept influencing education, psychology, and modern AI systems.

## 📙 Task 3: Bayes’ Theorem in Real Life

### Overview
This project explains Bayes’ Theorem and demonstrates its real-life applications in healthcare, 
artificial intelligence, law, and prediction systems.

### Formula
P(A|B) = [P(B|A) × P(A)] / P(B)

### Applications Covered
- Medical Diagnosis
- Email Spam Filtering
- Criminal Investigation & DNA Evidence
- Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
- Weather Forecasting
- Everyday Decision Making

### Importance
- Supports decision-making under uncertainty
- Used in AI and predictive models
- Reduces probability misinterpretation
- Connects mathematics with real-world problems

### Learning Outcome
Developed understanding of:
- Probability theory fundamentals
- Bayesian reasoning
- Applications in modern AI systems
- Statistical thinking in real-life situations
